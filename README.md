# FiltR

### AI-powered candidate screening and interview intelligence

FiltR is an AI-powered recruitment and interview intelligence platform that turns resumes and adaptive video interviews into structured, evidence-based hiring insights.

Instead of making automated hiring decisions, FiltR helps recruiters understand **what a candidate claims, what they can demonstrate, and what evidence supports each requirement**.

---

## What FiltR Does

FiltR connects the recruitment workflow from job requirements to interview evidence:

**Job Description → Candidate Resume → AI Analysis → Adaptive Interview → Evidence → Recruiter Review**

### Recruiter

- Create and manage jobs
- Upload job descriptions
- Extract structured requirements using AI
- Add candidates and upload resumes
- Extract candidate profiles from resumes
- Compare candidate information against job requirements
- Generate personalized interview questions
- Review interview transcripts and evidence
- Review structured interview reports

### Candidate

- Access an interview through a secure interview link
- Complete camera and microphone checks
- Participate in an AI-powered video interview
- Answer role-specific questions
- Receive adaptive follow-up questions when additional evidence is needed
- Complete the interview and submit responses
